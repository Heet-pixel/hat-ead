FAQ

Common Questions

How long does a website take? +
Most websites are delivered within 1–2 weeks depending on complexity. SaaS platforms and custom software may take 3–6 weeks. You'll get daily progress updates via WhatsApp throughout.
Do I own the website after it's built? +
Yes, 100%. All code, assets, and content belong to you after final payment. HAT gives you full ownership of everything we build.
What tech stack do you use? +
We use React, Next.js, Node.js, Express, MongoDB, PostgreSQL, and Tailwind CSS. Tech is chosen based on your specific needs — not convenience.
Can you help with design if I don't have a brand? +
Absolutely. We offer full UI/UX design services including logo, color palette, typography, and brand identity. You don't need to arrive with any design assets.
Do you offer hosting and domain setup? +
Yes! We handle deployment, domain configuration, SSL setup, and hosting. We support VPS, shared hosting, AWS, Vercel, and more.
What if I need changes after launch? +
HAT provides ongoing support and maintenance. Post-launch changes are handled promptly, and we offer monthly maintenance plans for regular updates.
